Vivapp is a fast, offline inventory app for field kitchens, military catering and large-scale food service. Replace paper tally sheets with QR code scanning and send a clean CSV stock list in seconds.

Built for kitchen managers, quartermasters, logistics staff and catering teams who need to count food provisions and kitchen supplies quickly and without errors – even far from any network.

★ KEY FEATURES

• QR code scanner – point the camera at a product label and the item is recognised instantly
• Scan from photo – read a QR code from an image in your gallery
• Built-in flashlight – scan in dark storerooms, tents and cold stores
• Product catalogue – search by name, SAP number or category and add items without scanning
• Quantity control – adjust counts with +/– and see what is already on your list
• Inventory list – review, correct, undo or clear your stock count at any time
• CSV export – share the list by e-mail, messaging apps or cloud storage for supply chain processing
• Works offline – the product catalogue is stored on the device, no internet needed to count stock
• Four languages – English, Deutsch, Français, Italiano
• Guided walkthrough and in-app Help & FAQ – get started in under a minute

★ WHY VIVAPP?

• Cut inventory and stock-taking time compared with paper lists
• Avoid typos in SAP numbers and quantities
• One simple workflow: scan → confirm quantity → send CSV
• Clean, modern design that is easy to use with gloves and under time pressure

★ IDEAL FOR

• Field kitchens and mobile kitchens
• Military logistics and ration / provision management
• Emergency response and civil protection units
• Catering companies, canteens and event kitchens
• Anyone who needs a simple food inventory and stock counting tool

Vivapp is open source and collects no personal data. Start your next stock count in seconds.
